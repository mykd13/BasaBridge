## About BasaBridge {#about}

BasaBridge helps students practise reading English stories out loud and answering questions about them. Levels, tries, and results follow fixed rules in code. The AI Teacher explains results and never decides them.

## Your account {#account}

A learner picks a username and a password and keeps them private. A learner under 13 needs a parent or guardian to approve use of the microphone. [Awaiting qualified review: who may open an account and on what terms.]

## How to use it {#rules}

- Read the stories as written and answer questions yourself.
- Do not use the app to harm others or to try to break it.
- Choose a username that has no real name or personal details.

## Stories and results {#content}

Stories are written by AI and checked by code before a learner sees them. Results and levels are a learning aid. They are not an official test score.

## Parents and guardians {#parents}

A parent or guardian can link to a learner's account when the learner shares a code. The learner chooses what is shared and can stop at any time. Parents never receive audio.

## Changes {#changes}

When these terms change, their version number goes up and signed-in users are asked to read them again.
