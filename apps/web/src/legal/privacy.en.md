## Who we are {#who}

BasaBridge is a web app that helps students read English stories for meaning. A learner reads a short story out loud, code compares what was said with the text, and the learner answers questions. Parents and guardians are optional.

## What we collect {#collect}

- **Account:** a username, a scrambled copy of the password, and an age band (not a birth date).
- **Email (optional):** only for password reset. Under 13, a parent or guardian email receives an approval request.
- **Reading:** the words heard, timings, scores, answers, and ability estimates.
- **Voice:** the recording is checked and then discarded. If the learner turns on keeping recordings, it is kept for 30 days and then deleted.

## Why we use it {#why}

We use this data to run the account, to check the reading, to choose the next story, and to show progress. We do not sell it and we do not use it for advertising.

## How long we keep it {#keep}

Account and reading data stay until the account is deleted. Voice recordings are discarded after analysis unless the learner turned retention on, then they are deleted after 30 days. [Awaiting qualified review: periods after a learner stops using the app.]

## Children {#children}

A learner under 13 needs a parent or guardian to say yes before the microphone is used. Until then the learner can read silently and answer questions. Parents never receive audio, and a learner chooses what a linked parent can see. There is a [privacy summary written for kids](/privacy/kids).
