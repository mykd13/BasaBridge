How tries work

A try is one read-aloud of the story. After each try the AI Teacher shows what to fix, and the child can read again.

When the tries are used up, the best try counts, the child answers the questions, and then gets a new story at the same difficulty.

A story only counts if the child reads to the end.

How a level is passed

A story passes when the child reads 85% of the words right on the counted try and answers at least 3 of 5 questions correctly.

Passing 3 stories at a level moves the child up one level. Very low results move them down one level so stories stay readable.

These numbers are the starting rules. They can be tuned as real results come in.
