Lesson · Thinking about the story

Why did it happen?

Goal: find out why something happened in a story.

The trick

Look for the words because, so and that is why. They point to the reason.

Example

Mia did not go outside. It was raining hard, so she stayed home and read.

Why did Mia stay home? Because it was raining hard.
